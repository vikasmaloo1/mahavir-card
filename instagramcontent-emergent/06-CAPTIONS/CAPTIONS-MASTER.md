Why your bright screen blue prints a little duller — RGB vs CMYK, explained simply.

Screens mix light (RGB); presses mix ink (CMYK). Convert early and on purpose so you approve the colour, not the machine. Save it for your next design handoff.

### C3 — Why bleed matters
**Format:** Carousel (5) · `03-CAROUSELS/c3-page-0X.png`
**Caption:**
That thin white edge on a cheap print job? Missing bleed.

Cutting blades move by fractions of a millimetre. Extend your background 3 mm past the trim and keep text 4–5 mm inside — no white slivers, no reprints. The 3 mm rule, saved.
**CTA:** We'll check bleed before we print — mahavircard.in
**Hashtags:** Set E → `#PrintTips #Prepress #DesignForPrint #GraphicDesignTips #Ahmedabad`
