# Weekly Frame — 20-Second Overlay Guide

Turn the "On the Press This Week" frame into a finished post in about 20 seconds, using a **real photo/video** of a job you actually printed.

See `on-the-press-EXAMPLE.png` in this folder for a worked example (clearly marked **EXAMPLE** — replace it with your own work).

---

## The 20-second method (Instagram app — no other tools)
1. **0–5s · Pick media.** Snap one clean photo (or a 3–8s video) of this week's job on the press or counter. Good light, tidy frame.
2. **5–10s · New post/story → add your media**, and pinch-zoom so it fills the middle of the screen.
3. **10–15s · Add the frame on top.** Tap the sticker/photo tool → add `on-the-press-story.png` (for a Story) → stretch it to full screen. The header, dashed area and footer sit over your photo; your photo shows through the middle.
4. **15–20s · One-line caption.** Replace the caption line with what you printed — e.g. *"1,000 visiting cards · thermal matt · out for dispatch."* Post.

> For a **feed** post, it's usually cleaner to post your real photo as the image and use `on-the-press-story.png` as a matching Story that points to it.

## The tidy method (Canva / CapCut — 2 minutes)
1. New design at **1080×1350** (feed) or **1080×1920** (story).
2. Layer 1: your real photo/video.
3. Layer 2: the frame PNG on top, full-bleed.
4. If you want the photo edge-to-edge inside the dashed box, delete that dashed rectangle in Canva first, then position your photo in that gap.
5. Type your one-line caption over `[ One line: what you printed this week ]`. Export.

## Worked example (what "good" looks like)
- **Photo:** a stack/fan of the finished cards, or the sheet on the press.
- **Caption line:** `5,000 leaflets · 170 GSM art paper · dispatched to Rajkot`
- **Post caption:** *On the press this week — [what it was] for [type of business]. Got a job to run? mahavircard.in · +91 94263 71150*

## Rules
- Only show work you **actually did** this week. No stock, no reused photos passed off as new.
- Keep the caption factual (product · finish · quantity · where it went). No exaggeration.
- Blur or crop any client details you don't have permission to show.
