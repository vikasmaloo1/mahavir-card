# 15 · Weekly Template — "On the Press This Week"

A reusable frame for a recurring weekly post/story where you drop in a **real photo or short video** of a job you actually printed that week. Ships with an empty placeholder area — no stock or generated imagery.

## Files
| File | Format | Use |
|------|--------|-----|
| `on-the-press-feed.png` | Feed 1080×1350 | Weekly feed post frame |
| `on-the-press-story.png` | Story 1080×1920 | Weekly story frame |

## How to use it (two easy ways)
**A — Overlay in any editor (Canva/CapCut/Instagram):**
1. Add your real photo/video as the bottom layer.
2. Place this PNG on top — the dashed area is where your media shows through if you first delete that rectangle, or simply position your photo to fill that zone and keep the header/footer.

**B — Simplest:** post your real photo/video as the post, and use this frame as a matching **Story** cover that links to it.

Add a one-line caption in place of `[ One line: what you printed this week ]` — e.g. *"5,000 visiting cards, thermal matt, out for dispatch."* Keep it factual; only show work you actually did.

Regenerate the frame from `_SOURCE/build-addons.mjs` (`15-WEEKLY-TEMPLATE` block in `addons.mjs`).
