# Mahavir Card — Instagram Growth Playbook

Practical, honest tactics for a real printing business. No follower-buying, no pods, no fake engagement, no "viral" promises. The goal is **business outcomes** — enquiries, quote requests, website clicks, and trade relationships — not vanity numbers.

## What we measure (business, not vanity)
Track these in Instagram Insights weekly:
- **Profile visits** and **website/link taps** (intent)
- **DMs started** and **quote requests** (enquiry)
- **Saves** and **shares** (the two signals that actually expand reach)
- **Reach from non-followers** on Reels (new-audience growth)
- **Location-tagged reach** (are we reaching Ahmedabad?)

A post that gets 40 saves and 3 DMs beats a post with 400 likes and no enquiry.

---

## Reels — the main reach engine
- **Hook in 1–2 seconds.** Put the keyword on screen in the first frame ("1,000 visiting cards", "RGB vs CMYK").
- **Design for completion.** Short (12–18s), clear beats, a reason to watch to the end ("…and here's the finished card").
- **Real process = retention.** Printing, cutting, foiling and peeling are inherently satisfying — lean into it, film clean close-ups.
- **Caption for search.** First line = plain keywords ("commercial printing in Ahmedabad"). 2–3 hashtags only.
- **Repurpose.** Turn top carousels into Reels; turn a Reel's best 5s into a Story teaser.

## Carousels — the save/authority engine
- Slide 1 is a hook, not a title card. Slide 2 must reward the swipe.
- One idea per slide, short text, logical progression, useful CTA on the last slide.
- Educational carousels (bleed, CMYK, paper guide) earn **saves and shares** — ask for the save in the caption ("Save this before your next order").

## Feed posts — product & trust
- Alternate product beauty shots with proof/behind-the-scenes. Don't make every post a sale.
- Strong single image + one confident line. Let the printing do the talking.

## Stories — daily presence & enquiry
- Post most days: polls ("Matt or gloss?"), questions ("What should we print next?"), finished jobs, and simple CTAs.
- Use **poll / question / quiz stickers** — replies and votes signal the algorithm and start DMs.
- Save the best into **Highlights**: `Website`, `Cards`, `Premium`, `Stationery`, `Stickers`, `B2B`, `FAQ`, `Contact`.

## Local relevance (Ahmedabad)
- Tag the **location** (Khadia Golwad / Ahmedabad) on every post.
- Use 1–2 hyper-local hashtags, not just generic `#printing`.
- Put **city + industry keywords** in the profile name and bio (search reads these).
- Show local landmarks/context honestly (the shop, the neighbourhood) to build "these are real, nearby printers" trust.

## B2B / trade content (a real growth lane)
- Speak directly to designers, agencies, resellers and small printers: "keep the client, we handle production."
- Post the outsourcing checklist, turnaround times, capacity (500–50,000+) and pre-press standards — these convert professionals faster than consumers.
- Make the DM path frictionless: "Send product + quantity + deadline and we'll quote today."

## Collaborations & community (organic, honest)
- **Local designers & agencies:** offer to print sample sets; co-post the result (tag each other). Their audience is your buyer.
- **Local businesses you print for:** ask permission to feature the finished job; tag them. Genuine social proof.
- **Customer-generated content:** invite clients to tag @mahavircard2011 when they receive an order; repost with credit.
- **Reply, don't broadcast:** answer every comment and DM quickly and usefully. Response speed is a real trust and ranking signal.

## Posting consistency & repurposing
- A sustainable rhythm beats bursts: ~4 grid posts + 2 Reels + daily Stories per week (see the 30-day calendar).
- Batch-shoot Reel footage once a week at the press.
- One idea → three formats: a tip becomes a carousel, a Reel and a Story.
- Refresh the evergreen educational carousels every quarter.

## Do
- Keep facts accurate (GSM, MOQ, turnaround) — trust is the whole business.
- Ask for saves/DMs with a clear, single CTA.
- Answer enquiries fast, in a human voice.

## Don't
- ❌ Buy followers or likes · ❌ engagement pods · ❌ spam comments/DMs
- ❌ fake reviews or fake conversations · ❌ irrelevant trending hashtags
- ❌ promise "viral" results · ❌ post a weak piece just to "post daily"

> Growth here looks like: more people in Ahmedabad and the trade knowing exactly what Mahavir Card prints, trusting the quality, and knowing it takes one DM or one visit to mahavircard.in to get a quote.
