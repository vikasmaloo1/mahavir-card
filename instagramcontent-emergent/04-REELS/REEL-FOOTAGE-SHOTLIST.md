# Mahavir Card — Reel Footage Kit (one-day phone shoot)

Film all 8 Reels in a **single 60–90 minute session** at the Khadia Golwad press. One phone, one small tripod, natural light near the window + one desk lamp. Shoot everything **9:16 (vertical), 1080×1920, 30fps**. Capture 8–10 seconds per shot even if you only use 3 — extra footage saves a re-shoot.

## Before you start (5 min)
- [ ] Wipe the phone lens. Lock exposure/focus (tap & hold on the subject).
- [ ] Clear clutter from the frame — keep only what's being filmed.
- [ ] One clean, uncluttered surface near the window = your "hero table".
- [ ] Keep a stack of finished cards, a sheet with colour bars, a loupe, a guillotine/cutter, sticker sheets, a brochure, envelopes, and a couple of boxes ready.
- [ ] Turn OFF flickering tube-lights if possible; daylight looks better.

## Universal B-roll to grab first (covers 80% of every Reel)
Shoot these 12 clips once — they feed almost every script:
1. Slow pan across a fresh sheet with **CMYK colour bars / registration marks**.
2. **Loupe** or hand moving over registration marks (close-up, no face needed).
3. **Guillotine cut** — blade coming down on a stack (side angle).
4. **Riffle/fan** a stack of finished cards with your thumb.
5. Macro **tilt across a gold-foil edge** catching light.
6. Macro of **raised spot-UV / velvet** surface.
7. **Peeling** one sticker off a printed sheet.
8. Sticker **applied** to a box or jar.
9. **Brochure fold** opening in hand.
10. **Envelopes/letterheads** squared into a neat pile.
11. **Boxes** being closed / courier label going on (dispatch).
12. Phone **screen-record** of mahavircard.in: home → pick product → paper/finish → live price → confirm.

> Tip: for each clip, start still (1s), do the motion slowly (3–4s), end still (1s). Slow = premium.

## Editing (any free app: CapCut / IG editor)
- Trim each clip to 2–4s; cut **on the beat** of a calm, modern track (no loud trending audio).
- Add the on-screen keyword in the **first frame** (Archivo/bold, white with slight shadow).
- Keep text in the **centre safe zone** (avoid top/bottom ~250px — IG UI covers them).
- End every Reel on a 2s hold: logo + `mahavircard.in`.
- Export 1080×1920. Post with the caption + 2–3 hashtags from the captions/hashtags files.

## Do / Don't
- **Do** film real work, slowly, in good light. **Do** shoot extra.
- **Don't** stage fake machines/people, don't use garbled on-screen text, don't over-edit with flashy transitions.

> If a clip doesn't come out well, you can still post the rendered **cover** as a static Reel, or convert the matching carousel pages into a slideshow Reel.
